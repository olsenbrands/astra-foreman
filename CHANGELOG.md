# Changelog

All notable changes to Astra Foreman are documented here.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-09-05

- Defaults substantive authorized delivery to qualified builders through
  implementation, tests, routine repair, integration, and permitted release work.
- Clarifies worker discretion, escalation boundaries, repair ownership, bounded
  lead takeover, and whole-delivery-cost decisions.
- Strengthens lead acceptance around real candidate evidence, critical
  user-facing/integration behavior, gaps, and disputed findings.
- Adds the public hero asset and refreshes package documentation.
