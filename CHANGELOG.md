# Changelog

All notable changes to Astra Foreman are documented here.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-09-07

- Strongly prefers an early qualified different-family challenge for substantial
  plans, with reasoned exceptions and targeted cross-family implementation review.
- Selects qualified model-and-tool combinations across authorized families and
  harmonizes builder and reviewer guidance without fixed provider castes.
- Makes early pilots exercise the task's promise and most consequential uncertainty.
- Requires terminal writers before review freeze and reopens affected criteria
  after source changes.
- Establishes one authoritative current checkpoint, links review-guard and raw
  evidence records, and adds elapsed time and precise usage distinctions at close.
- Clarifies private-preview distribution while the public setup page is a waitlist.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-09-05

- Defaults substantive authorized delivery to qualified builders through
  implementation, tests, routine repair, integration, and permitted release work.
- Clarifies worker discretion, escalation boundaries, repair ownership, bounded
  lead takeover, and whole-delivery-cost decisions.
- Strengthens lead acceptance around real candidate evidence, critical
  user-facing/integration behavior, gaps, and disputed findings.
- Adds the public hero asset and refreshes package documentation.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-05

- Initial public release.
